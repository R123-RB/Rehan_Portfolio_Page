Sleep Health & Human Wellness Study

An interdisciplinary statistical study examining the relationship between sleep patterns and overall human wellness indicators. Using R and Jamovi as primary tools, the project applied regression modelling, ANOVA, and correlation analysis to quantify how sleep quality and duration influence physical and mental health — producing results interpretable by both technical and non-technical audiences.

1

Identified statistically significant correlations between sleep duration and key wellness metrics across the study population

2

Applied Jamovi's ANOVA and correlation modules to rigorously test null hypotheses and validate statistical conclusions

3

Produced visual reports with regression outputs and charts accessible to non-technical stakeholders

4

Demonstrated a full research pipeline: data collection, analysis, interpretation, and structured reporting

02 Data Analysis July 2025

Titanic Survival Data Analysis

A comprehensive exploratory and inferential analysis of the Titanic dataset, investigating survival patterns across socioeconomic class, gender, age, and passenger demographics. The project combined statistical methods with data visualisation to uncover which factors most strongly predicted survival, producing structured findings and publication-quality graphics from raw historical data.

1

Revealed clear survival disparities across passenger classes through quantified, visualised comparisons — class 1 vs class 3 survival rates clearly demonstrated

2

Applied feature engineering and data wrangling to handle missing values and encode categorical variables appropriately

3

Generated histograms, heatmaps, and box plots illustrating key survival patterns in a clear, publication-ready format

4

Produced a structured analysis report translating statistical findings into accessible, well-supported conclusions
